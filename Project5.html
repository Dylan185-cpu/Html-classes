Facts about cats

Cats are known for their unique features like a unique nose print and three eyelids, and their impressive physical abilities, such as jumping up to six times their height and reaching speeds of 30 mph. They communicate with humans through a variety of sounds like meowing, but rarely meow at other cats. Cats are also known for sleeping for a large portion of their lives and have a sophisticated sense of balance and low-light vision.

Studies about cats cover a wide range of topics, including the scientific discipline of felinology, which is the study of cats. Recent research includes a genetic study of cat domestication history, a study on how cats respond to human emotions, and an analysis of how they communicate with their owners, showing that cats meow more at men because men tend to be less verbally attentive. Other research investigates cat behavior, cognition, and welfare, using methods like citizen science, genetic sequencing, and GPS tracking.
